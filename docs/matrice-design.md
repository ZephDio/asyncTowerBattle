As you know the game is about creating an army grid, with defense, offense, and economy.
Let's enum the strategy :

There is the defensive strategy, where you like the game going for the long run, it can be in many form, like accumulating buffs for units or towers and scale better than the opponent.

You can go for an offensive strategy, by sending many units fast and take advantage of food being full at the start of combat, the goal would be to kill the opponent before the scaling get out of end.

In both of those strategy there are many way to reach  it : 
You can attack the food economy of your opponent, waiting for him to run out of ressources and winning by the malus it provides.

You can stack furry, as it is a polynomial bonus, starting to increase your furry before your opponent can give you the edge when the furry buff scale.

You can remove opponent army buff, a midd/late game strategy could be to reduce opponent scaling while maintaing yours to get the edge.

For supporting those strategy you can have synergy withing "tags", for exemple we could have a totem giving a buff to a specific unit type for making your strategy better.

As well as spesific mechanique, like debuffing units enemy, traps, units with projectils etc.

Each game should encourage to cross one two or three axes, so every board feel fresh (this is opptistic but the end goal will be there)
