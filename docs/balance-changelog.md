# Balance changelog

## Balance patch 1

| Piece | Old | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elf (arrow rate) | every 3 s | every 4.5 s | Elf arrows were too strong. |
| Red Obelisk (cooldown) | 5 s | 8 s | Fury was triggered too often. |
| Red Obelisk (price) | 10 | 20 | Matches its Legendary power. Shop share stays 10. |
| Bosquet (Animal castle damage) | +1 | +0.5 | Animals hit the castle too hard. |
| Dwarf (summon time) | 1.6 s | 3.5 s | Slower unit production. |
| Elf (summon time) | 1.2 s | 3.5 s | Slower unit production. |
| Dwarf and Elf castles (production speed) | x1 | x2 | Partly offsets the longer summon time: the castle summons its default unit every 1.75 s (was 1.2 s for the Elf castle, 1.6 s for the Dwarf one), so castles are slower overall, the Elf one by about 46%. Barracks use the 3.5 s pace. |

## Balance patch 2

| Piece | Old | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fury (start) | 20 s | 30 s | Fury ended battles too early. |
| Fury (interval) | every 2 s | every 3 s | Slower ramp, same triangular growth (1, 3, 6, 10...). |
| Red Obelisk (cooldown) | 8 s | 9 s | Playing it was close to an instant win. |
| Red Obelisk (synergy) | +10% cast speed per Evil piece | +5% | Same reason. |
